6. Browser Settings Reference Guide

Step-by-step instructions for resetting blocked camera and microphone permissions by browser:

Google Chrome / Microsoft Edge (Chromium)
1. Look at the left side of the address bar at the top of the browser.
2. Click the Site Settings (tune/lock) icon: 🔒 or 🎛️.
3. Find Camera and switch it from "Block" to "Allow".
4. Find Microphone and switch it from "Block" to "Allow".
5. Click the Reload button in Chrome or press Ctrl+R / Cmd+R.
Mozilla Firefox
1. In the address bar, look for the crossed-out camera or microphone icon.
2. Click the icon, then click the "✕" next to Blocked or Blocked Temporarily.
3. Reload the page and select "Remember this decision" when the prompt asks.
Windows / macOS System Permissions
Windows 10/11: Open Settings ➔ Privacy & Security ➔ Camera (and Microphone). Ensure "Camera access" is ON and "Let desktop apps access your camera/microphone" is ON.
macOS: Open System Settings ➔ Privacy & Security ➔ Camera (and Microphone). Ensure your browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) is toggled ON.
